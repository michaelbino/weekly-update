Attendance Information

As we begin another school year, please be aware that the District Attendance Committee worked diligently throughout the summer, to align schoolwide practices, create more consistent intervention strategies, and identify meaning ways to improve student attendance. The main goal is to minimize lost instructional time and to maximize student engagement in the classroom.

New Jersey state standards consider nearly all absences, regardless of whether a parent note or documentation is provided, as unexcused. The District Attendance Committee has updated a few of the attendance codes in Genesis to reflect these standards. You may notice this on your child's Daily Attendance.

How to Report an Absence or Tardy

Parents are requested to enter their child's attendance in Genesis directly through the Parent Portal!

A "Telesafe" or dedicated attendance phone line will no longer be in use.

To access this feature, simply log in and:

  1. Click on “Attendance” in the left-hand toolbar.
  2. Select “Notify our Office.”
  3. On the page, you can specify the student's absence, choose a date range, and if you have multiple children in the district, you can indicate which one will be absent.
  4. A space is provided for you to leave a brief explanation for the absence.

For step by step directions please click here.

We encourage you to take advantage of this new "Notify Attendance Office" feature for a more direct and immediate response.

Should you need any assistance or have questions about your parent portal account, do not hesitate to contact our front office staff at (609) 671-5521, or your child's school counselor.

PaySchools Instructions

Please follow these Simple Steps to Manage your Students' Lunch Account

  • Visit PaySchoolsCentral.com or download the PaySchools Central Mobile app, available on Google Play or Apple App Store.
  • Once registered, you will receive an email to finish the account details: set up a password, link student(s), choose notifications, and set up a payment method.
  • From the dashboard, you can review the meal and fees balance, add account restrictions, and opt to pay all or partial balances, set up auto replenishment, and more.

Free/Reduced Lunch

Families who have participated in the free and reduced lunch program in the past are reminded to please go online to the Parent Portal in Genesis to complete a new application. The main office has hard copies of the application if you prefer. A new application should be submitted each school year however, only one application per family is necessary. Families who do not submit a new application in a timely manner will be dropped from the program in mid-October (more detail as we move closer).

For questions related to lunch applications, and eligibility benefits please reach out to the main office at 609-671-5500 or office@example.org.
